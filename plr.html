Slide 3

If your doc has #notes, it is considered a presentation. Presentations have this sidebar with the fullscreen button.

Save the presentation (with enter), open a tab with the saved file on the primary screen and another one on the projector, fullscreen the projector one. They will sync.

If you dont have notes, the doc will be rendered as a list of pages. If your screen is wide enough, it will fit 2 or 3 pages at once, then you need to scroll.
